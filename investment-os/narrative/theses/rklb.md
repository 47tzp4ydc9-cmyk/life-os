# RKLB thesis

## Summary

Rocket Lab is the only credible Western pure-play small-launch + medium-launch provider outside SpaceX. Defense and national-security demand is structural. Premium-selling around the position (short puts at multiple strikes/expiries) generates income while the company executes toward Neutron commercialization.

## Why this works

- Government and defense customers need launch capacity that is not SpaceX. RKLB is the default second source.
- Neutron is the inflection — moves RKLB from $40-100M revenue per launch to direct competition for medium-lift contracts.
- Premium-selling on dips: when RKLB drops, IV spikes (~90%+), making cash-secured puts a high-edge way to add exposure at lower effective cost.

## What kills it

- Neutron program slip beyond 12 months — competitive window narrows as Blue Origin and others mature.
- Capital raise at distressed prices (<$60) — dilution + signal of execution stress.
- Sustained launch failures damaging customer trust.
- Sector rotation out of speculative growth — short puts get crushed even without RKLB-specific bad news.

## What I'm tracking

- Quarterly launch count and revenue per launch.
- Neutron milestones (test fires, first launch date).
- Defense contract announcements — proxy for backlog quality.
- Short-interest and options IV — when both spike, that's the premium-selling window.
- All open short puts (Aug21 105P, Sep18 105P, Oct16 110P, Oct16 145P) — running positions in [narrative/options/](../options/).
