# GLW (Corning) thesis

## Summary

Corning is the large-cap, stable, dividend-paying anchor for the optical/photonics theme. Direct beneficiary of AI data center buildout via optical fiber and connector demand. Less torque than AAOI/LITE/COHR but durable cash flow and dividend cushion.

## Why this works

- Hyperscaler AI buildout requires massive amounts of fiber + connectors — Corning is a default supplier with decades of optical glass leadership.
- Premium-selling around it works (the Aug 175P closed +$950 on Jun 24) — IV pops on tech selloffs let cash-secured puts get in at lower effective cost.
- Anchor position role: stable enough to size up, not so boring that returns are dead.

## What kills it

- Datacom segment revenue stalls for 2 consecutive quarters — implies AI optical demand is plateauing or a major customer shifted suppliers.
- A major hyperscaler signals an AI capex pause — would deflate the whole optical cohort, GLW included.
- ASP compression on optical connectors from new entrants — margin story breaks.

## What I'm tracking

- Quarterly datacom revenue and segment margin.
- Hyperscaler capex commentary (MSFT, GOOGL, META, AMZN).
- Optical connector pricing trends — proxy for competitive intensity.
- Entry status — wanted 200 shares around $210-215; stock ran to $223+ on Jun 25, may need to wait for pullback or sell a CSP at $210-215 strike.
