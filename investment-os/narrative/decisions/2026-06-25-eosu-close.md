# Sell 58 EOSU @ $24.00 — close position

## Why

Bought 58 @ $25.03 avg on Jun 24 intending a short-term upside play on an EOSE bounce. Two days in, three signals lined up to exit:

1. EOSE rights offering Jul 1 — 6 days out. Arb traders typically short into the record date, capping or reversing pre-event bounces.
2. Daily 2x decay had already eaten the position: $25.03 → $23.34 in 48h, vs a smaller proportional drop in the EOSE underlying.
3. Better capital uses available same day (SMCI, NVO, ANET — all with cleaner catalysts).

## Chart context

EOSU traded $22.10–$28.80 the day of entry (Jun 24). Closed Jun 25 at $23.69 — flat-to-down despite EOSE underlying not moving meaningfully. Same-day SOXL was +10% (Micron-driven semis rally); EOSU not catching that bid was the tell.

## What I'm wrong about if this fails

EOSE gaps up >15% before Jul 1 on a contract or tech announcement that overpowers the rights-offering arb — EOSU could've doubled. Acceptable miss given the asymmetric decay risk on continued holding.
